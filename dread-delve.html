For Chance — who never let a dungeon stay boring.
Beneath Saint Andral's church the font is still running, and what runs down into the silver mine is not water. Build the descent, drop the horrors in, and let the table walk it together.

How to play

Camera — drag to orbit · right-drag or two-finger drag to pan · scroll or pinch to zoom
Move — tap a token, then tap a glowing tile. In combat the glow is that creature's remaining speed. Closed doors open as you walk through; heroes stop short of traps they spot.
Doors & traps — tap a door or a found trap for its card: open, pick, force, disarm. Search looks for hidden ones within 15 ft.
Stairs — stand on a stair rune and press Descend / Ascend on the token card.
Ping — the ◎ button, or long-press a tile, flashes it for the whole table.
Attack (DM) — select a creature, press an attack (◆ toggles advantage/disadvantage), tap the target. The roll pops up for everyone.
Shift — rolls the d100 Shift table: corridors rewire, rooms swap, stairs wander, doors lock, things spawn. At Dread 6+ it happens on its own at round's end.
Grasp Die — Andral's hands. It grows when hands die; roll it for what reaches up out of the floor.
Build (DM) — the Build tab: drag rooms, drag corners to resize, draw rooms on bare rock, trace corridors, place doors, traps and stairs. Ctrl+Z undoes.
