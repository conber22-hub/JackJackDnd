# THE DEAD VILLAGES · Twelve Scenes

Each building can carry one of three stage descriptions. The captions are original, DM-only suggestions and are **not** automatically revealed to players.

| Location | Inhabited | Abandoned | Infested |
|---|---|---|---|
| Svalich cottage | An iron kettle rocks beside the fire | The chimney has been plugged from inside | The wooden threshold bulges when someone knocks |
| Peat-cutter's hut | Damp boots steam outside the door | A child's coat remains on the peg | Rootlets curl inside both empty sleeves |
| Timber longhouse | A chopping block still holds a fresh log | Every axe has been buried blade first | The timber walls creak in a single shared rhythm |
| Palisade watch house | One guard rings the hour | The bell rope moves without a bell | An eye opens in the bell's empty housing |
| Morning chapel | Sunlight barely touches the final candle | The altar cloth is laid over something human-shaped | Cysts spell a prayer nobody in the chapel has heard |
| Last Candle Inn | Music stops when the travelers arrive | There is a warm cup on a cold table | The upstairs windows blink one after another |
| Mourner's ossuary | A mourner quietly sweeps stone dust | Every inscription has been scraped away | The erased names reform as ridges in bone-white growth |
| River granary | Grain rains through the loft slats | A millstone turns with no belt attached | Spores rise from the grain in the shape of hands |
| Covered market hall | Bread is stacked on a clean cloth | Every coin has been pinned to a stall door | Something under the stall fabric breathes with the wind |
| Bell schoolhouse | A slate bears tomorrow's lesson | The chairs are arranged facing a locked closet | Small knocks answer any name spoken in the room |
| Charcoal workshop | The smith has banked the coals for night | A hammer rises, then falls, without a hand | Red iron fungus grows from the extinguished hearth |
| Burgomaster's manor | A dozen windows welcome visitors | Portraits face the wall even after being turned around | The roof grows horn-like spires facing Castle Ravenloft |

**Return-visit rule:** On a new visit, rerender the same building with its later stage. Do not alter its x/y/w/h, doorway, road access, or the encounter state. A discovered building must not disappear just because a cosmetic effect changes.
