# The Road Watches · DM Scene Cards

These are **original optional horror encounters** for JackJackDnd. Nothing here automatically changes the game rules. Each scene has three discrete cues; the DM decides when to reveal each. A successful party should receive more information, not a forced jump scare.

## The Last Light Inn

**Type:** false-shelter · **Best locations:** roadside, village · **Minimum Gaze:** 1

**First sight:** The inn has one warm window. Its curtains are drawn.

**When they investigate:** There is no fireplace behind the window. The warmth comes from beneath the floorboards.

**On returning:** On returning, the sign bears the name of a missing traveller.

**Set dressing:** Main GLB `last_light_inn.glb`; nearby pieces: `abandoned_funeral_cart.glb`, `last_lantern_sign.glb`, `handprint_shutter.glb`.

## Running the horror

- Show the location from a distance before the reveal. A normal road gives the party something safe to retreat toward.
- Let every discovery be voluntary. Do not force camera pans, character actions or saving throws.
- Use silence before a single sound, not a permanent audio wall. Provide captions and a reduced-motion alternative.
- If the party returns, change one detail, not the whole map. Familiarity makes small differences frightening.
- Hunger Moss is visual until the DM explicitly adds collision, attacks, or other mechanics. Keep protected routes, stairs and doorways clear.
