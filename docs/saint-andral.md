# Running St. Andral’s in Vallaki

Chapter 5, area N1, refers to the village church in Chapter 3, area E5, for
its room arrangement. This original VTT layout uses a central entry hall
with four side rooms, a nave and rear belfry, and a fenced graveyard
beside the church. The resting place is a small crypt beneath the altar.
See [the Vallaki chapter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/cos/the-town-of-vallaki)
for the investigation, the church’s protection and the Feast.

| Entry | What the party gets |
| --- | --- |
| **St. Andral’s Church** in the template picker | Church and churchyard above a small crypt. Starts at the front steps. |
| **Go inside** at the named Vallaki facade | The same church and crypt after the Vallaki exterior PR lands. Return through the Maps shelf. |
| **Beneath Saint Andral’s (Vallaki)** from the Valley or Session 8 | The same surface plan with Bloom dressing, then the existing undercroft, Silver Vein, Hand-Warrens and Undertow. |

Saved church maps keep their old floor plans. Make a fresh copy to try the
new layout. The surface and the campaign delve are separate saved maps.
The floor beside the altar is represented by a concealed door and a small
stair landing. Use normal secret-door controls to reveal it when the
party investigates or Father Lucian shows them the way.

## Table beats

- **Before evening prayers.** Put Lucian in the nave, Yeska in the vestry
  and Milivoj at work in the yard. The parish office gives the party a
  place to ask Lucian about the bones away from the congregation.
- **Follow the people.** Lucian can point the party toward Yeska. Let
  Yeska’s concern lead them to Milivoj and the churchyard. Milivoj can
  send them to Henrik’s shop when he decides to talk.
- **During the Feast.** Keep the main aisle clear so the congregation can
  flee down the steps. If that door is held, send them out the side door
  and through the churchyard gate. Treat the belfry above the mapped
  landing as a point of entry in the scene.
- **In Flood Edition.** Put the Seep near the marked font and the Count’s
  creatures on the approaches. The grate near the altar leads into the
  campaign’s undercroft after Lucian opens it.

The named NPCs, the state of the church’s protection and the timing of
the attack are run by the DM. The two entrances and the concealed access
use the VTT’s existing door and movement rules.
