# Running the Blue Water Inn

Select **Blue Water Inn** in the template picker to start on the front porch.
Once the authored Vallaki exterior lands, the facade’s **Go inside** button
uses the same layout and the normal map shelf. The template creates a separate
copy for rehearsal. An inn already saved on the shelf still has its saved layout.

The map follows the arrangement of rooms in
[*Curse of Strahd*, Chapter 5, area N2](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/cos/the-town-of-vallaki).
The floor plans and room descriptions are original and compressed for VTT play.

| Floor | Places to play |
| --- | --- |
| Ground | The porch leads to the taproom. The bar serves the room, while the kitchen has a delivery door to the stable yard. The wine store, stable and tack room each have their own door. |
| Upper | The stair leads to the guest gallery. The innkeeper can unlock guest rooms for the party. A concealed service passage branches toward the family rooms and the ravens’ loft. |
| Attic | A concealed door off the family rooms leads to the hatch landing. The bird opening is part of the scene description. |

## At the table

- **First visit.** Put Danika at the bar and Urwin in the kitchen. Let the
  party choose between talking with the hosts, asking the wolf hunters about
  the road or staying upstairs.
- **On a return.** Show the state of the winery deliveries through the casks
  in the wine store. If the party helps, move a few fresh barrels in and let
  Danika acknowledge who made it possible.
- **A reason to head out.** Rictavio’s horse is stabled here. A conversation
  about him can point the party to his wagon at the Arasek Stockyard across
  town.
- **After hours.** Move conversation to a quieter corner and close the public
  doors when it fits the session. The room notes are prompts for the DM.
  Named characters and their schedules are set at the table.

The VTT supports one shared stair tile between adjacent levels. The public
stair links the taproom and gallery, while the hidden landing represents
access to the attic. The stable loft is reached through the private passage
on the upper map. The gallery implies the open space over the taproom with
its footprint. The layouts use the existing closed, locked and secret door
states so the DM can reveal or open access with normal controls.
