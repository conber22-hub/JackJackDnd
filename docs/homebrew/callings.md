# Dread Delve's own rules: the Callings of the Mists

*In memory of Chance, "Dutcher", the Dutch Oven, who loved Halo and history.*

Dread Delve is growing out of D&D into its own game. The story stays: Barovia, the Count, the Seep (this campaign's rot, our parallel to the Flood), the sessions of *Curse of Strahd: Flood Edition*. What changes is **what you build a hero from**: the classes, subclasses, feats, species and backgrounds become our own. This page is the shared picture. Claude, ChatGPT and Muse each build a part of it (see "Who builds what" at the end).

## The premise: the Mists take from every age

The Mists have always pulled people into Barovia. In this telling they take from **every age**. A hoplite from a phalanx at dawn, a longbowman who never came home from France, a Norse household guard, a steppe rider. And soldiers from wars that haven't happened yet: armoured, shielded, carrying battered relics of a far-future war that no smith in the valley can mend. They wake in the fog with the gear they had and the habits that kept them alive.

That's the blend: **Halo's soldiers and history's warriors, dropped into gothic Barovia.** The tech is old and failing, the history is bloody and real, and the Count doesn't care where you came from.

## Rules that don't change

The **d20 core stays**: a d20 to hit and to save, armour class, hit points, the six abilities, proficiency, conditions, advantage and disadvantage, levels 1 to 20, and spell slots for those who cast. Everything already built (fights, captions, the dice moment, the bestiary, the screens) keeps working.

## What a Calling is

A **Calling** replaces a D&D class. Each one stands on one of the twelve existing classes, its **chassis**. The chassis provides hit dice, saves, armour, spell slots and the mechanics the engine already runs. The Calling gives it:
- its own name, story and look (colour, emblem);
- its own name and words for every feature (the Vanguard's *Field Patch* is the Fighter's *Second Wind* underneath);
- new mechanics where they make it its own (the Vanguard's recharging **Ward**, the Seepbound's pact with **the Chorus**);
- three **Paths** (subclasses). The third Path of every Calling is **Seep-touched**: power from the rot, at a price. These are our Flood parallels.

A hero keeps `cls` (the chassis: `'Fighter'`) and gains `calling` (`'vanguard'`), so every save already made still loads.

## Origins (in place of species) and backgrounds

An **Origin** says where and when you're from. Each is the d20 traits a species gives (speed, size, darkvision, a resistance, a once-a-rest trick), with our own names:
- **The Drawn**, by age: *Ancient* (bronze and the phalanx), *Medieval* (steel and the oath), *Far-Future* (armour, a failing suit, relic gear).
- **Born in Barovia**: villager, Vistani-raised, of the old blood (the Count's line, at a distance).
- Stranger kin: the Dusk-folk (elf-like, long memory), the Hill-born (dwarf-like, stubborn), and more as we find them.

**Backgrounds** are what you did before the Mists: soldier, field medic, archivist, smuggler, scout, smith…

## Feats

Our own, built around the soldier's life and the valley, for example: *Hold the Line*, *No One Left Behind*, *Steady Hands*, *Second Breath*, *Old Habits*, *Relic Tinker*, *Seep-Scarred*…

## Spells

Spells stay as they are for now. A later round will rename and rework them (an Archivist's are *Protocols*, a Chaplain's *Rites*, a Seepbound's *Gifts of the Chorus*).

## Dutcher, and the Dutch Oven

This game is made **in memory of Chance**, whom we called Dutcher, or the Dutch Oven. He loved Halo and history, and the Callings above are built from both. He's in the game three ways:
1. **The dedication**: a quiet page when the game opens, and in the credits.
2. **The Dutch Oven**: a bakehouse-forge in the Village of Barovia, the warmest hearth in the valley. Its great iron oven has never gone cold. Heroes rest there, and wake there.
3. **The legend of Dutcher**: a soldier the Mists drew in long ago. He built the oven, fed the village through the worst winter, and held the line when the Seep first came. His helmet hangs over the oven's mouth, and his cairn stands on the hill above the village. The village remembers him in songs, notes and a toast.

Claude writes the memorial pieces; the DM checks every word.

## Hard lines (all of us)

- **Original names only.** Inspiration comes from roles and feel, never from names. Nothing that belongs to the Halo games: none of its factions, species, characters, ships, vehicles, named weapons, places or catchphrases. History is everyone's: hoplites, huscarls, yeomen and the pankration are fair game.
- Gothic first: whatever the age, it's Barovia, and it's grim.
- The Seep-touched Paths always cost something.
