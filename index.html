How to play

Camera — drag to orbit · right-drag or two-finger drag to pan · scroll or pinch to zoom
Move — tap a token, then tap a glowing tile; or drag the mini along the way you want it to go (back over your route to shorten it). With a mouse, hovering a tile shows the way and its cost. ←↑→↓ or the number pad step one square. Closed doors open as you walk through; heroes stop short of traps they spot. ↶ Undo move takes a move back (players: only if it revealed nothing).
In a fight — the bright glow is the move you have left, the dim red-orange glow needs the Dash (walking there takes it). The card has 🏃 Dash, 🛡 Disengage, 🌀 Dodge and ⤒ Stand up (half your speed). Leaving an enemy's reach stops you at its edge: the enemy's player (or the DM) chooses Strike or Let them go, then the walk carries on. A red dot on the path warns you first.
Attacks and spells — pick one on the card and the map shades how far it reaches (bright: normal range, dim: long range, at disadvantage). With a mouse, hovering a target draws the lines from your corner to theirs: green clear, gold past a creature, red through a wall — half cover is +2 AC, three-quarters +5, total cover can't be targeted. A ranged attack with an enemy beside you, or at a prone target far off, has disadvantage. Area spells follow the pointer (on a phone: tap to place, tap again to cast) and ring everyone they'd catch — friends included. Walls stop them. Players can't take shots they can't make; the DM can, with a second tap.
Minis — heroes, allies and humanoid foes are forged 3D figures built from their sheets: folk, armour, weapons and shield all show. Shape your hero in the creator's Looks (drag the figure to spin it; ⚔ ✨ 🚶 to see it move) and take your portrait from it with 📸. DMs: 🎲 Roll an NPC in the Tokens tab.
Moving the party (DM) — outside a fight, the heroes nearby follow the one you move, single file in marching order (Tokens tab: ⇡ reorders, 🚩 Stays keeps one put, 🚶 Party follows turns it off). Shift-click creatures (or 👥 Select party / Select its kind) to move several at once in formation. 🐾 Close in walks a monster up to the nearest hero.
Maps (DM) — the Maps tab keeps every map you leave on a shelf, as the party left it: fog, doors, furniture and whoever stayed behind. Session dungeons and Valley places come back off the shelf when you return. ⇢ Go there takes the party; ✎ Set it up opens a map without them (the players' screens hold still under a veil) until you 📍 bring the party here (tap where they arrive) or go ↩ back to them. 📍 Move the party regroups everyone on a square you tap. Outside a fight, the party follows whoever takes the stairs.
Starting a fight (DM) — ⚔ Start a fight… ticks the party and the creatures in sight, close by or alerted (the rest of the floor stays out), can let identical creatures share one roll, and sets who is surprised (disadvantage on initiative). In the fight: 🤖 Its turn runs a monster's turn (hit, shoot, or close in and hit), ⏭ holds back the current creature, ✕ takes one out of the fight. When every foe is down, the win is called with its XP.
Game night (in person) — the DM's Night tab opens the table screen: a window to drag onto the TV. It shows what the party sees: whose turn it is across the top with the heroes' faces, the party along the bottom, and a camera that follows the action. From the Night tab the DM can:
  • put read-aloud text, a face and a name, or a handout picture on the TV
  • turn the lights out
  • send the TV where the DM is looking
  • run a turn timer
  • turn on real dice, then type in the d20 the players rolled
🎲 Ask the table for a roll (any save or check, DC shown or secret): it pops up on the players' phones for their real d20 (or "roll for me"); you type or roll for anyone without one; then the TV reveals everyone's result at once, group check included. With real dice on, the fight picker takes the players' initiative rolls too, and the TV tumbles a d20 for every attack. 🤫 Pass a note buzzes one player's phone with something only they see (optionally in Strahd's voice, and the TV can show that a note changed hands). 📜 Previously on… fills the read-aloud with the story so far from the log, to trim and show. Phones buzz on your turn, and the TV marks who's up next.
Players can join on their phones (scan the QR on the Table tab, or put the 📲 Join card on the TV) and turn on 📇 Card only: no map, just their hero, big buttons, a list of targets, and a pad to step one square (north is up, as on the TV). The TV screen and card-only phones stay awake.
📚 Your books — the DM can import the compendium export from their character app (Table tab → Your books). It stays in that browser, never on the website. Its species, backgrounds, feats, subclasses and spells join the character creator (players' phones get them when they join the table), its creatures join the Beasts tab, and anything in it can be looked up there. The app's own rules win a name clash, and library spells roll what their description says plainly.
Doors & traps — tap a door or a found trap for its card: open, pick, force, disarm. Search looks for hidden ones within 15 ft.
Stairs — stand on a stair rune and press Descend / Ascend on the token card.
Ping — the ◎ button, or long-press a tile, flashes it for the whole table.
Playing together — the DM opens a table (Table tab) and sends the invite link or the code; players in the room can scan its QR code instead. If a phone sleeps or the DM reloads, everyone reconnects on their own, back on the same hero. There's nothing to accept: a player who knocks comes straight in. If they're stuck on Knocking… (the DM sees "can't get through" in the Table tab), their network won't let the two devices talk directly: add a free relay in Table tab → Relay, press Test the relay, then send the invite link again.
Attack — select a creature, press an attack or spell (◆ toggles advantage/disadvantage), tap the target. The roll pops up for everyone. Players do the same with their own hero: the DM approves the roll (or turns on Players roll freely in the Table tab).
Slots & features — casting spends a spell slot. With more than one slot level left you choose which: a higher slot makes most spells stronger (Fireball at level 5 rolls 10d6, Magic Missile adds a dart, Scorching Ray a ray). 🌀 Concentration: casting a concentration spell drops the one you were holding; taking damage calls for a Constitution save (DC 10 or half the damage, the app rolls it), and being incapacitated or killed ends it. When it ends, whatever it was holding (Hold Person's paralysis, Web's grip) lets go. The ✕ on the 🌀 chip lets go on purpose. Rage, Second Wind, Lay on Hands, Hit Dice and the rest are buttons on the hero's card. Short and long rests are in the Tokens tab; making camp on the road is a long rest.
Legendary & lair — a legendary creature's card counts its legendary actions; in its lair a 🏰 button appears in the initiative list each round.
Noise (📢) — fights, forced doors, traps and thunder wake things nearby and on the floors above and below.
Rooms — the first time the party enters a room, the DM is offered its read-aloud. The Dungeon tab can read any room aloud, furnish it or clear it.
Search — next to a chest, coffin, desk, bookshelf or wardrobe (and more), Search opens it and shows what's inside, finds hidden stashes in the furniture beside you, and spots secret doors within 15 ft.
Furniture — tap a piece, or the 🪑 chips on a hero's card for what's beside them. Open chests, coffins, wardrobes and drawers (a sarcophagus lid needs Strength), pick or force a lock, push a crate or table a square (heavy things need Athletics), topple a shelf or statue onto whoever's behind it (Dex save or damage and prone), or smash it (its AC and HP follow the rules for objects; breaking things is loud). A shelf in front of a secret door swings aside once the door is found. DMs: the card sets a lock, what's inside and a hidden stash, and ↺ puts a piece back.
Difficult terrain (Build → ≋ Rough) — mud, rubble and floodwater cost double to cross.
Strahd's Gaze (top bar, 1–10) — how closely the Count is watching. Higher Gaze: thicker mist, redder light, his creatures fight harder, the dead get up, and from 8 his voice charms a hero each round.
Whim Die (🦇) — how he toys with the heroes. It grows when his creatures fall and when they catch his eye; roll it for a raven, a gift, wolves, a letter, a charm… or him.
Shift — rolls the d100 Shift table: corridors rewire, rooms swap, stairs wander, doors lock, things spawn. At Gaze 6+ it happens on its own at round's end.
Props (Build tab) — tables, a bar, barrels, carriages, trees and more. Tap to place, tap again to turn. Minis climb up onto low ones and stand on top (tables, crates, beds: difficult terrain), hop fences, and walk round tall ones (shelves, statues, pillars, hearths).
Levelling up — at 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th and 19th level the creator offers an Ability Score Improvement or a feat (Alert, Grappler, Magic Initiate, Skilled, a Fighting Style… or write in any other).
Build (DM) — the Build tab: drag rooms, drag corners to resize, draw rooms on bare rock, trace corridors, place doors, traps and stairs. Ctrl+Z undoes.
